Carry every guarantee the contract states into the documents with its reason
and the tool or review that verifies it, or tell the driver which guarantee is
dropped and why.

Why: Once the contract is retired, a guarantee missing from the documents is
lost to every reader who does not search the history, and a rule without its
reason or its checker is the first to be broken.
