Repository files state intent: what holds and why. Never record verification
runs, counts or revisions something was checked against; CI and prek verify. A
contract's criteria and the baseline SHA/branch of a contract's Visuals diagram
are the only exceptions.

Why: Recorded verification goes stale immediately and adds noise without adding
assurance.
