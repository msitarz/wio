State a quantitative requirement with its threshold and the workload and
measurement conditions needed to interpret it, and a qualitative one with a
verification method and its scope instead of a number.

Why: A threshold without its workload cannot be reproduced, so it can neither
pass nor fail, and an invented number gives a false sense of precision where a
method can actually be applied.
