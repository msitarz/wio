Keep what several children share in their parent, and what one Work owns, its
local context and the implementation details it decides, in that Work.

Why: Inheritance gives shared context to every child, while a copy in each child
drifts, and a parent that predefines its children's details takes decisions away
from the stage that has the context to make them.
