Name each criterion's guarantee in its heading, after its id, and start the
criterion with one sentence that says what becomes possible or changes when it
is delivered.

Why: Records and reviews cite criteria by id, the heading tells a reader what
the id stands for, and the first sentence tells a reviewer what to look for.
