Define a term in the Glossary only when its meaning in this Work could be read
more than one way, reuse and link the meaning a term already has in the
repository instead of defining it again, and leave the section out when there is
nothing to define.

Why: Obvious entries cost tokens in every session and hide the ones that matter,
and two definitions of one term drift apart until agents implement whichever
they read last.
