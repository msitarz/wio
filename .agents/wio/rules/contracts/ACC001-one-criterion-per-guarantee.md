Give each guarantee that can be assessed on its own its own criterion, keep the
scenarios that establish one guarantee together, and make the criteria together
fulfil the purpose and the goals, without aiming for a number of criteria.

Why: A record or a review can then say exactly which guarantee it addressed, and
a target count invents or merges criteria.
