Put every requirement in the acceptance criteria or in the Constraints section,
never in the mechanisms or the additional context, and keep constraints apart
from the Work's own criteria.

Why: Reviews and records cite criteria and constraints, so a requirement
anywhere else is never checked, and constraints are inherited by children while
criteria are not.
